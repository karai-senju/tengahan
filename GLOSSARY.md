# Tengahan

Helps a small group of friends pick a fair place to hang out, between where each of them lives.

## Language

**Peserta**:
One person in the group, with a name (optional) and a home location. A group has 2 to 5 Peserta.
_Avoid_: user, friend, member

**Lokasi Rumah**:
The point on the map a Peserta starts from, usually their home.
_Avoid_: address, origin

**Titik Tengah**:
The geographic middle of all Lokasi Rumah, measured as straight-line distance on the map, not by roads or travel time.
_Avoid_: midpoint, center, meeting point

**Tempat Nongkrong**:
A cafe or restaurant near the Titik Tengah that the group could meet at. Malls are not Tempat Nongkrong.
_Avoid_: venue, spot, POI

**Radius Pencarian**:
How far from the Titik Tengah Tempat Nongkrong are looked for; starts at 2 km and widens when nothing is found.

**Perkiraan Waktu Tempuh**:
A rough travel time from a Lokasi Rumah to a Tempat Nongkrong, worked out from distance alone and ignoring traffic.
_Avoid_: ETA, route time

**Link Bagikan**:
A link that shows the Titik Tengah and its Tempat Nongkrong to whoever opens it, without revealing any Lokasi Rumah.
_Avoid_: share URL, permalink
